# Privacy Policy for Blox Suite

**Effective date:** August 18, 2026
**Last updated:** August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Blox Suite (the "Extension," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Extension on roblox.com. This policy is intended to comply with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies and applicable data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), where applicable.

By installing and using Blox Suite, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Overview

Blox Suite is a browser userscript that adds optional display and convenience features to the Roblox website. It is designed to operate with minimal data collection. This document explains, in full, every category of data the Extension accesses, why it is accessed, where it is sent, and how long it is retained.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information stored locally on your device

The Extension stores your feature preferences (such as which features are enabled and any custom values you configure) using the browser's userscript storage API or, as a fallback, `localStorage`. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us as part of normal operation.

### 2.2 Information transmitted to our infrastructure

The Extension sends a periodic status request, referred to internally as a heartbeat, to a server that we operate. This request occurs automatically at intervals of approximately two minutes while the Extension is active on roblox.com. The heartbeat includes the following data:

- Your Roblox user ID
- Your Roblox display name
- The version number of the Extension

This information is used solely to estimate the number of active users of the Extension. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond aggregate usage measurement. We do not collect your password, session tokens, payment information, private messages, or trade contents.

### 2.3 Information requested from third-party services

Depending on which features you enable, the Extension makes requests directly from your browser to the following third-party services:

- **Roblox Corporation** (users.roblox.com, thumbnails.roblox.com, presence.roblox.com, and inventory.roblox.com), to retrieve publicly available profile, presence, and inventory information
- **Rolimons** (api.rolimons.com and rolimons.com), to retrieve publicly available item valuation data

These requests are made directly from your browser using your existing authenticated session. They do not pass through our servers, and we do not log, store, or have access to the responses returned by these services. Your use of these third-party services is subject to their own respective privacy policies.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described in Section 2.2 exclusively to:

- Estimate the number of active users of the Extension
- Monitor adoption of new Extension versions

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information to third parties. We do not use collected information for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Retention

Heartbeat data described in Section 2.2 is retained only for as long as necessary to calculate aggregate usage statistics and is periodically purged. We do not maintain an ongoing profile associated with your Roblox user ID beyond this aggregate use.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share the information described in this policy with third parties, except:

- Where required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
- Where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Extension, its users, or the public

## 6. User Control and Choices

You may disable individual features of the Extension at any time through its settings interface. Disabling a feature stops the associated third-party requests described in Section 2.3. The heartbeat described in Section 2.2 does not currently have a dedicated in-extension toggle; it can be stopped by disabling or uninstalling the Extension.

You may clear all locally stored preferences at any time by resetting the Extension's settings or clearing your browser's site data for roblox.com.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through the Extension, please contact us using the information in Section 11 so that we may take appropriate action.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information described in this policy from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Visual-Only Features

Certain features of the Extension, including but not limited to display of a custom balance value, a verified badge, or custom friend entries, alter only the visual rendering of the Roblox website within your own browser. These features do not modify your actual Roblox account, balance, verification status, or friends list, and are not visible to other users.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Extension's functionality or applicable legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the Extension after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 11. Contact Information

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the data practices described herein, you may contact us through the Extension's official repository on GitHub.

## 12. Open Source Disclosure

Blox Suite is open source software. The complete source code is publicly available for review, allowing independent verification of the data practices described in this policy.
